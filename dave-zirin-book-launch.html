About the Book

The People's Historian

HARDCOVER · PENGUIN RANDOM HOUSE · ON SALE AUG 4, 2026

Drawing on Howard Zinn's family papers, Dave Zirin traces the life behind A People's History of the United States — the four-million-copy bestseller that reshaped how generations of readers see American history. The book follows Zinn from a working-class Brooklyn childhood through the Depression, WWII, the civil rights movement, and the fight against the war in Vietnam, up to his death in 2010.

Publishers Weekly gave the book a starred review, calling it a vivid portrait of the historian who reshaped how Americans understand the country's potential for change. — Publishers Weekly
